# Hawi's Arcade 🕹️

A tiny neon mobile game arcade. Open the hub, pick a game, and play!

## Play it

**On your phone (GitHub Pages):** `https://itx-hawi.github.io/prompt-generator/`

**Locally:** download the HTML files and open them in any mobile or desktop browser. No internet needed.

## Games

- **Neon Flappy** (`flappy.html`) — tap to flap through the neon gates. Start menu with options (sound on/off, difficulty: easy/normal/hard), best-score tracking, and sound effects.
- More games coming soon — the hub (`index.html`) is ready for them.

## How to play Neon Flappy

- **Tap / click / Spacebar** — flap
- Don't hit the gates, the ground, or the sky
- Your best score and settings are saved on your device

Built with plain HTML5 Canvas + WebAudio — zero dependencies, works offline.
