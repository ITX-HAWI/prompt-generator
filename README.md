# Neon Flappy

A tiny neon mobile game. Tap to flap through the gates — how far can you get?

## Play it

**Option 1 — GitHub Pages (recommended, play on your phone):**
1. On github.com, open this repo → **Settings** → **Pages**
2. Under "Build and deployment", set Source to **Deploy from a branch**, Branch to **main**, folder to **/(root)**, then Save
3. Wait a minute, then open: `https://itx-hawi.github.io/prompt-generator/`

**Option 2 — locally:** download `index.html` and open it in any mobile or desktop browser. No internet needed.

## How to play

- **Tap / click / Spacebar** — flap
- Don't hit the gates, the ground, or the sky
- Your best score is saved on your device

Built with plain HTML5 Canvas — zero dependencies, works offline.
